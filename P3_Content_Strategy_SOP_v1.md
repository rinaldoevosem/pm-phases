**PURPOSE & SCOPE**

This SOP defines the standard process for planning, creating, and
organizing all written and visual content required for the website. It
ensures that content is strategically aligned with the client's brand,
target audience, and SEO goals before any design layouts are finalized.

**Phase Objective**

Deliver a complete, client-approved content package — including page
copy, CTAs, meta data, image direction, and content map — that the
design and development teams can use without delay or ambiguity.

**Scope Includes**

> \- Content audit of existing website (if applicable)
>
> \- SEO keyword research and content strategy
>
> \- Sitemap and content map creation
>
> \- Page-by-page copywriting (headlines, body, CTAs)
>
> \- Meta titles and descriptions for all pages
>
> \- Image and media direction per page
>
> \- Content review and client approval process
>
> \- Content handoff to design team

**Scope Excludes**

> \- Visual design or layout (covered in P4 - Branding & Design)
>
> \- Product descriptions for inventory (covered in P5 - Inventory
> Management)
>
> \- Blog post writing (unless included in project scope)
>
> \- Social media content creation
>
> \- Photography or videography production

**HOW - PROCESS STEPS**

> **1. Conduct Content Audit**
>
> If the client has an existing website, audit all current pages and
> content. Document: what content exists, what is usable, what needs
> rewriting, what is missing, and what should be removed. Identify
> content gaps and SEO opportunities. If no existing site, skip to
> keyword research.
>
> **2. Perform SEO Keyword Research**
>
> Research primary and secondary keywords for each target page. Analyze
> competitor content and keyword rankings. Identify long-tail
> opportunities and content themes. Document target keywords per page
> with search volume, difficulty, and intent classification.
>
> **3. Create Sitemap and Content Map**
>
> Build a comprehensive sitemap showing all pages and their hierarchy.
> For each page, define: purpose, target keywords, content sections,
> CTAs, and internal linking strategy. This becomes the blueprint for
> both content creation and design layout.
>
> **4. Write Page Copy**
>
> Create copy for every page defined in the content map. Each page
> should include: H1 headline, section headings (H2/H3), body copy,
> calls-to-action, and any feature/benefit lists. Write in the client's
> brand voice. Ensure keyword integration is natural and strategic.
>
> **5. Draft Meta Titles and Descriptions**
>
> Write SEO-optimized meta titles (under 60 characters) and meta
> descriptions (under 160 characters) for every page. Include target
> keywords naturally. Ensure each is unique and compelling for
> click-through from search results.
>
> **6. Create Image and Media Direction**
>
> For each page, document image requirements: hero images, section
> images, icons, videos, or animations needed. Include direction on
> style, mood, and subject matter. Reference brand guidelines. Note
> which images the client is providing vs. which need to be sourced or
> created.
>
> **7. Internal Content Review**
>
> Have the content package reviewed internally by the PM and design
> lead. Check for: brand voice consistency, SEO optimization,
> completeness (no missing pages or sections), alignment with project
> requirements, and feasibility for design layout.
>
> **8. Client Content Review**
>
> Present the full content package to the client for review. Walk
> through the content map and key messaging decisions. Collect feedback
> page by page. Allow up to 2 rounds of revisions on copy.
>
> **9. Finalize and Hand Off Content**
>
> Incorporate all approved changes. Organize the final content package
> in Google Drive with clear folder structure (by page). Notify the
> design team that content is ready. Update ClickUp tasks. Post in Slack
> confirming content handoff.

**REVISION LIMITS & SCOPE CONTROL**

Page Copy: Up to 2 rounds of revisions included; additional rounds
billed at hourly rate

Meta Data: 1 round of revisions included

Content Map / Sitemap: Structural changes after approval require PM
sign-off and may impact timeline

*Any additional revisions beyond the limits above require a Change Order
approved by the Project Lead and communicated to the client with
associated timeline and cost impact.*
